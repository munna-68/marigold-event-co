# Marigold Event Co. — Design Direction

## Three initial approaches

| Theme Name | Very Brief Intro | Probability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Harbor Blueprint | A restrained coastal-blue operating system for celebrations, using editorial photography and quietly technical booking cues. It makes rental logistics feel considered rather than transactional. | 0.07 |
| Garden Afterlight | A dusky botanical approach where event installations glow against deep blue space, balancing locality with a sense of occasion. | 0.04 |
| Playful Precision | A brighter modular system with crisp geometry and confident yellow accents, designed to make extensive inventory feel easy to explore. | 0.09 |

## Chosen approach — Harbor Blueprint

**Design Movement.** Harbor Blueprint draws from contemporary wayfinding, service design, and understated editorial hospitality photography. It treats Marigold as a capable local partner with a polished operational point of view.

**Core Principles.** The interface remains calm around high-stakes choices; the booking system gives specific status cues rather than vague reassurance; moments of warmth are concentrated in gold light and photographs; and every surface is both spacious and utilitarian.

**Color Philosophy.** Deep ink navy establishes dependability and makes the gold details read like lights coming on at an event. Fog blue and salt-gray create room for dense information without the habitual warm-cream/terracotta treatment. Marigold is deliberately scarce: it signals affirmation, a selected date, and the customer’s next best action.

**Layout Paradigm.** Pages use an offset field-guide composition. A narrow operational rail holds navigation, date and quote actions while wider content areas shift between photography, inventory, and booking states. The home page uses an asymmetrical hero and stacked horizontal editorial bands rather than a centered card grid.

**Signature Elements.** A thin gold route line appears at section handoffs and quote milestones; compact “field labels” pair dates, item counts, and service notes; and curved tent-arch outlines quietly frame key image corners and confirmation states.

**Interaction Philosophy.** Controls acknowledge logistical decisions with concise state changes: availability responds immediately, quote totals stay in view, and selection is always visible. Interactions should feel like a calm dispatcher assisting a host, not an animated checkout funnel.

**Animation.** Page entrances use a single 280ms opacity-and-translate reveal with 45ms card staggering. Buttons compress to 0.97 on press, selection rails slide 160ms with a custom ease-out, and all nonessential motion disables under reduced-motion preferences. No looping ornamental motion or dramatic parallax is used.

**Typography System.** `DM Sans` carries navigation, controls, and operational details in precise medium weights. `Newsreader` is reserved for high-impact display headlines in a soft, editorial italic/roman pairing. Headlines remain left-aligned and spacious; numbers use tabular DM Sans where totals and dates matter.

**Brand Essence.** Marigold Event Co. is the meticulous neighborhood rental partner for hosts who want an event to feel effortless, not improvised. Personality: **assured, considerate, organized**.

**Brand Voice.** Headlines are observant and specific; CTAs are active and practical; microcopy anticipates questions before they interrupt planning. Example lines: “A better event starts with the pieces that make it run.” and “Check the tent, then build the whole day around it.” Generic welcoming language and empty superlatives are avoided.

**Wordmark & Logo.** The mark is an open tent arch intersected by a six-petal marigold—an icon for shelter, occasion, and a bright finishing touch. The wordmark uses a custom-feeling wide DM Sans treatment with a small marigold dot, not a default font presentation.

**Signature Brand Color.** **Marigold Signal — `#E7B329`**: a confident, sunlit yellow reserved for vital status and action.

## Style Decisions

The gold route line and open tent arch act as a recurring milestone language at browse entry, item availability, quote building, and request confirmation. The inventory grid retains a field-guide tone through a persistent category rail and operational, per-item calendar cues. Additional category-specific photography and varied crops prevent the catalog from reading as a sparse placeholder collection.

## Content integrity

This portfolio demo uses original business copy and fictional inventory data. It does **not** present fabricated customer reviews, ratings, or testimonials. Credibility is conveyed through service standards, clear deposit terms, delivery coverage, and transparent booking mechanics.
